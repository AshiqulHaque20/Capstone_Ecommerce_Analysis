\# 📊 **E-commerce Customer Segmentation using RFM Analysis**



\## 📌 Project Overview



This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior in an e-commerce business and segmenting customers using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis.



The main goal is to identify high-value customers, understand purchasing patterns, and uncover opportunities to improve customer retention and overall business performance.



\---



\## 🎯 Problem Statement



E-commerce businesses often struggle with retaining customers and identifying their most valuable users.



This project aims to:



\* Analyze customer purchase behavior

\* Identify high-value and at-risk customers

\* Provide actionable insights to improve retention and revenue



\---



\## 🛠️ Tools \& Technologies Used



\* \*\*SQL (PostgreSQL)\*\* → Data extraction, joins, and RFM analysis

\* \*\*Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)\*\* → Data analysis and visualization

\* \*\*Power BI\*\* → Dashboard creation and business storytelling



\---



\## 📂 Dataset



\* Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset (Olist – Kaggle)

\* Includes data on customers, orders, payments, and products



\---



\## ⚙️ Workflow



1\. \*\*Data Preparation (SQL)\*\*



&#x20;  \* Joined multiple tables (orders, customers, payments, products)

&#x20;  \* Cleaned and aggregated data

&#x20;  \* Created RFM metrics (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)



2\. \*\*Customer Segmentation\*\*



&#x20;  \* Assigned RFM scores using SQL window functions

&#x20;  \* Segmented customers into:



&#x20;    \* Champion

&#x20;    \* Loyal Customer

&#x20;    \* Big Spenders

&#x20;    \* New Customer

&#x20;    \* At Risk

&#x20;    \* Lost Customer

&#x20;    \* Others



3\. \*\*Data Analysis (Python)\*\*



&#x20;  \* Analyzed customer segments

&#x20;  \* Compared average spending across segments

&#x20;  \* Visualized customer distribution



4\. \*\*Dashboard (Power BI)\*\*



&#x20;  \* Built an interactive dashboard showing:



&#x20;    \* Customer distribution

&#x20;    \* Revenue by segment

&#x20;    \* Key KPIs (customers, revenue, average spend)



\---



\## 🔍 Key Insights



\* A large number of customers fall into low-engagement segments like \*Others\* and \*Lost Customers\*, indicating poor retention.

\* \*Lost Customers\* have the highest average spending, meaning high-value customers are being lost.

\* \*New Customers\* show strong spending behavior, suggesting effective acquisition but weak retention.

\* Revenue is not driven by loyal customers, indicating dependency on new or inactive users.

\* Significant opportunity exists in re-engaging at-risk and lost customers.



\---



\## 📊 Dashboard



A Power BI dashboard was created to visualize customer segmentation and revenue distribution.



It helps answer key business questions such as:



\* Which customers generate the most revenue?

\* Which segments need attention?

\* How is customer engagement distributed?



\---



\## 🧠 Conclusion



The business shows strong customer acquisition but struggles with retention.



A large portion of high-value customers become inactive over time, which impacts long-term growth.

Improving retention through loyalty programs, personalized marketing, and re-engagement strategies can significantly increase customer lifetime value.
