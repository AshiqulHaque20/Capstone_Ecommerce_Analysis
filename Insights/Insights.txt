E-commerce Customer Segmentation – Key Insights

---

## Business Insights

1. Overall Business Performance
   The dataset shows approximately 198,000 total orders and around 99,000 unique customers, generating total revenue of nearly 16 million. This indicates a moderate customer base with significant transaction volume.

2. Customer Purchase Behavior
   The number of unique customers is close to the total number of orders, suggesting that most customers make only one purchase. This indicates low repeat purchase behavior and highlights a retention issue.

3. Revenue Trend Over Time
   Monthly revenue shows a steady increase starting from early 2017, peaking around November 2017. However, a sharp decline is observed in September and October 2018, which may indicate missing data or a drop in business activity.

4. Top Performing Categories
   Health and beauty products generate approximately 1.25 million in revenue, followed by the watches and gifts category with around 1.20 million. This indicates that the business relies heavily on a few key product categories.

5. High-Value Customers
   A small group of customers contributes disproportionately to total revenue, showing the presence of high-value customers that are critical to the business.

---

## RFM Insights

6. Customer Distribution
   A large portion of customers fall into the "Others" and "Lost Customer" segments. This indicates that most users are either low-engagement or inactive, highlighting a major retention challenge.

7. High-Value Customers Are Being Lost
   The "Lost Customer" segment has the highest average spending, indicating that previously valuable customers are no longer active.

8. Strong Acquisition but Weak Retention
   "New Customers" show high average spending, suggesting effective customer acquisition. However, many do not return, pointing toward weak retention strategies.

9. Loyal vs High-Value Customers
   "Champion" and "Loyal Customer" segments tend to have lower average spending, meaning they purchase frequently but with smaller transaction values.

10. At Risk Customers
    Customers in the "At Risk" segment were previously active but are now becoming inactive, making them ideal targets for re-engagement campaigns.

11. Revenue Concentration
    A significant portion of revenue comes from non-loyal segments such as "Others" and "Lost Customers", indicating that revenue is not strongly driven by long-term loyal users.

---

## Final Conclusion

The business demonstrates strong customer acquisition and generates substantial revenue, but suffers from poor customer retention. A large number of customers make only one purchase, and high-value customers are being lost over time.

Improving retention through loyalty programs, personalized marketing, and re-engagement strategies can significantly enhance long-term revenue and customer lifetime value.
